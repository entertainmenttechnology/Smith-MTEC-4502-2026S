## **Objective**

Students will draft a tailored, professional cover letter for a specific employment opportunity, internship, or graduate program. The goal is to connect your background, technical skills, and strategic career narrative directly to the requirements of a real-world target position.

---

## **Assignment Steps**

### **1. Identify a Target Opportunity**
* Find a specific job posting, internship listing, or graduate program description relevant to your career goals.
* Save the full job description or URL as reference material.

### **2. Research the Organization & Requirements**
* Analyze the target position to identify key technical competencies, soft skills, and organizational values.
* Note 2–3 specific requirements where your experience or academic project work is strongest.

### **3. Draft the Cover Letter (1 Page / 300–400 Words)**
Include the following standard sections:
* **Header & Salutation:** Professional contact info, date, employer info, and named recipient (or appropriate title).
* **Opening Paragraph:** State the position applied for, how you found it, and a compelling 1-sentence hook introducing your professional focus.
* **Body Paragraph 1 (Relevant Technical & Creative Experience):** Highlight 1–2 specific projects or technical achievements directly matching the job requirements.
* **Body Paragraph 2 (Value Proposition & Alignment):** Explain why this specific organization appeals to you and how your work style and skills add value to their team.
* **Closing Paragraph:** Request an interview, thank the reader, and provide contact details.

### **4. AI Assistance & Refinement (Optional)**
* You may use AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude) to brainstorm hooks, align key terms with job requirements, or polish phrasing.
* **Important:** The final letter must reflect your authentic voice, verified facts, and personal experiences. Include an AI usage note if AI was used during drafting.

---

## **Deliverables**

1. **Cover Letter Draft (PDF or Markdown):** A polished 1-page cover letter.
2. **Target Job Description / Link:** Included as an appendix or linked in your submission.
3. **Short Reflection (150 words):** Briefly explain how you tailored your narrative to this position and what adjustments you made during drafting.

---

## **Evaluation Criteria**

* **Tailoring & Relevance:** Directly addresses the specific target role and company.
* **Structure & Clarity:** Follows standard business letter format; concise and clear.
* **Evidence of Value:** Highlights relevant projects, technical skills, and achievements.
* **Professional Tone:** Free of typos, grammatically sound, and appropriately confident.
