# Assignment 3: Exploring and Evaluating Portfolio Platforms

**Course:** MTEC 4502 – Career and Portfolio Seminar  
**Instructor:** Dr. David B. Smith  
**Due:** Week 7–8  

---

## Overview

Research professional portfolio platforms across creative, technical, and hybrid domains to determine which platform best fits your career goals, technical workflow, and budget.

---

## Assignment Steps

### Part 1: Research & Evaluation Matrix
Review **3–5 platforms** (e.g., Behance, GitHub Pages, Squarespace, Webflow, WordPress, Readymag, Cargo, Wix). Complete an evaluation table comparing:
* Target Users & Industry Standard
* Ease of Use vs. Design Control
* Hosting Cost / Free Tiers
* Media & Code Integration (interactive elements, video, GitHub links)
* Strengths & Weaknesses
* **Selection Decision (Y/N)**

### Part 2: Platform Selection & Portfolio Structure Outline
* Formally select your chosen platform and write a **150-word justification**.
* Outline your planned portfolio structure (e.g., Home/Bio, Resume, Featured Projects, Process Notes, Contact).
* Select 3–5 initial work samples or projects to showcase.
