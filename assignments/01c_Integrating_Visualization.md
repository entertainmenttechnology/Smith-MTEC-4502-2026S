# Assignment 1c: Integrating Visualization into Your Career Strategy

**Course:** MTEC 4502 – Career and Portfolio Seminar  
**Instructor:** Dr. David B. Smith  
**Due:** Week 4  

---

## Objective

Translate your written Strategic Framework (Part 1b) into a visual model. Visualizing your career path helps organize complex relationships between skills, timelines, and goal milestones, while providing an engaging format for peer feedback and portfolio presentation.

---

## Assignment Steps

### Step 1: Research Visualization Platforms
1. **Explore Platforms:** Research at least 3 visualization tools (e.g., MindMeister, Lucidchart, Miro, Trello, Coggle, Draw.io, Canva).
2. **Deliverable (150–200 words):** Write a brief platform comparison evaluating ease of use, design flexibility, collaboration features, and accessibility (free vs. paid tiers). State which platform you chose and why.

### Step 2: Select Diagram Structure
Decide which diagram type best represents your strategy:
* **Career Pathway Diagram:** Maps branching roles and advancement trajectories.
* **Skills Gap Radar / Chart:** Compares current proficiency against target requirements.
* **Gantt Chart / Timeline Roadmap:** Displays short-term and long-term milestones over time.
* **SWOT Matrix:** Evaluates Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats.

### Step 3: Produce Initial Visual Rough Draft
Create an initial visual draft incorporating:
* Major skill gaps to develop
* Actionable milestones (short-term & long-term)
* Estimated timelines and target completion dates
* Key tools and resources required

---

## Submission Requirements

1. **Platform Comparison Analysis (150–200 words).**
2. **Visual Strategy Rough Draft:** Shareable link, image file (PNG/JPG), or embedded diagram.
3. **Reflection (150 words):** Describe how mapping your framework visually clarified your career plan or surfaced unexpected challenges.
