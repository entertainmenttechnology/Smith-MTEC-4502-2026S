# Assignment 1b: Strategic Framework & Job Market Gap Analysis

**Course:** MTEC 4502 – Career and Portfolio Seminar  
**Instructor:** Dr. David B. Smith  
**Due:** Week 3  

---

## Objective

Build upon your baseline reflection (Part 1a) by conducting real-world job market research. You will analyze actual job postings and industry requirements to identify skill gaps, evaluate personal alignment, and outline actionable short-term and long-term milestones.

---

## Assignment Components & Deliverables

### Mandatory Research Requirement (Zotero)
Before gathering job listings, ensure you have installed the **Zotero Connector**. **All job listings, company profiles, and industry sources referenced in this assignment must be added to your Zotero library** and cited properly in your submission.

### Part 1: Job Requirements Analysis
1. **Identify Job Listings:** Research 3–5 job listings across entry-level, mid-career, and senior positions to map a full trajectory. Use platforms like LinkedIn, Indeed, or the [ENT Department Job Board](https://openlab.citytech.cuny.edu/groups/entertainment-technology-student-resources/docs/entertainment-technology-jobs-and-internships/). Save each listing to Zotero.
2. **Comparison Table:** Create a structured table comparing required qualifications, technical tools, responsibilities, and experience levels across these listings.
3. **Reflection (150 words):** Explain how these real-world requirements shape or modify your career expectations.

### Part 2: Skills Gap Analysis
1. **Compare External Data vs. Current Skills:** Compare the qualifications found in your job market research with your Part 1a self-assessment.
2. **Deliverable:** A table listing **Required Skills vs. Current Proficiency**, explicitly identifying gaps to be bridged. Add a 150–200 word reflection on your primary growth areas.

### Part 3: Personal Alignment & Resource Awareness
1. **Values Alignment (100–150 words):** Reflect on whether your target career path aligns with your personal values (e.g., work-life balance, creative control, collaboration, financial stability).
2. **Resource Constraints:** Identify realistic constraints regarding **Time**, **Money/Budget**, **Tools/Software**, and **Manpower/Network** needed to achieve your goals.

### Part 4: Actionable Goals & Timeline
1. **Short-Term Goals (6 Months – 1 Year):** 2–3 specific, measurable milestones (e.g., software certifications, portfolio projects, internships).
2. **Long-Term Goals (1 – 3 Years):** 2–3 career positioning objectives post-graduation.
3. **Timeline / Gantt Draft:** A visual or tabular timeline showing specific steps and target completion dates.
