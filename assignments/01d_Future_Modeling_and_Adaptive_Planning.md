## Assignment Components

### Part 1: Future of Work Stress Test Synthesis (500–750 Words)
Write a synthesis answering:
* How is your target role projected to evolve, hybridize, or fragment over the next 5–10 years?
* Which current skills may become obsolete or automated? Which emerging competencies will become essential?
* Cite at least **one Core reading** and **two independent Zotero sources**.

### Part 2: Revised Visual Strategy Map
Update your visual diagram from Assignment 1c to incorporate:
* Future-of-work trends and potential industry disruptions
* Adaptive contingency pathways (Plan B / hybrid role options)
* Updated skill acquisition milestones
