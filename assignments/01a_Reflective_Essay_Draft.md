# Assignment 1a: Reflective Essay Draft – Speculation & Self-Assessment

**Course:** MTEC 4502 – Career and Portfolio Seminar  
**Instructor:** Dr. David B. Smith  
**Due:** Week 2  

---

## Objective

Draft a reflective essay that serves as your baseline foundation for career goals, personal motivations, and skill self-assessment. 

**CRITICAL RULE:** **Do NOT use outside resources, job listings, or AI tools for this draft.** This exercise requires you to rely *exclusively* on your internal state, current knowledge, and personal reflection. Research and AI tools will be introduced in later steps, but you must first record your unassisted baseline.

---

## Guidelines & Required Sections

Your draft (750–1000 words) should be structured with the following headings:

### 1. Introduction to Career Aspirations
* Outline your long-term career goals and sectoral interests (e.g., sound design, interactive media, video production, creative coding, management).
* Describe the motivations behind your career choice and what activities bring you genuine fulfillment.

### 2. Tools, Skills, and Knowledge for Success
* Identify the specific technical tools, creative workflows, and domain knowledge you *believe* are crucial for success in your target area.

### 3. Self-Assessment (Strengths & Growth Areas)
* Provide a self-assessment of your current expertise. 
* Highlight your unique background strengths as well as specific technical or professional areas needing development.

### 4. Initial Strategic Thoughts
* Offer preliminary ideas on how you plan to address your skill gaps and leverage your strengths over the next 1–3 years.

---

## Submission Requirements

* **Format:** Shareable Google Doc link or PDF added to your student workspace folder (`student-work/firstname-lastname/assignments/`).
* **Length:** 750–1000 words.
* **In-Class:** Be prepared to discuss your reflection and key insights during our Week 2 session.
