# Assignment 5: Budgeting as Professional Practice

**Course:** MTEC 4502 – Career and Portfolio Seminar  
**Instructor:** Dr. David B. Smith  
**Due:** Week 13  

---

## Objective

Resource management is a core professional skill. This assignment expands budgeting beyond financial numbers to evaluate how **time, software/tool costs, equipment, labor, and personal overhead** affect career sustainability, freelancing, project production, and job transitions.

---

## Assignment Components

### 1. Financial & Operational Budget Artifact
Develop one of the following budget models based on your career trajectory:
* **Option A: Post-Graduation 1-Year Transition Budget:** Living expenses, software subscriptions, portfolio maintenance, gear/tools, emergency fund, and target income.
* **Option B: Freelance Rate & Production Budget:** Hourly/day rate calculation, overhead, taxes, hardware/software amortization, project expenses, and profit margin.
* **Option C: Creative Project / Grant Production Budget:** Equipment rental, labor/collaborators, venue/hosting, marketing, and contingency.

### 2. Resource Trade-Off Reflection (300–500 Words)
Write a reflection analyzing:
* **Time vs. Money:** How will you balance income-generating work against professional development and portfolio updates?
* **Tool & Software Investment:** Which tools are essential paid investments vs. open-source/free alternatives?
* **Manpower & Network:** How does collaboration reduce project costs or expand capacity?

---

## Deliverables

1. **Budget Spreadsheet / Table Artifact** (Excel, Google Sheets, PDF, or Markdown table).
2. **Resource Trade-Off Reflection Essay (300–500 words).**
