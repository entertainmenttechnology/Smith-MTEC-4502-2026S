## Overview

The final capstone deliverable of MTEC 4502 is your **Online Professional Portfolio** and **Timed Professional Pitch**. Your portfolio synthesizes your work throughout the semester into a live, professional digital artifact that communicates your technical capabilities, strategic career narrative, and personal brand to employers, clients, or graduate admissions.

---

## Portfolio Minimum Requirements

Your live portfolio site must include the following core sections:

1. **Homepage / Introduction:** Clear value proposition, professional title, and navigation.
2. **Professional Bio & Story:** Engaging summary adapted from your Reflective Essay.
3. **Featured Projects (Minimum 2–3):** Structured case studies displaying process, role, technical tools, and final results.
4. **Résumé & Cover Letter Access:** Embedded view or downloadable PDF versions.
5. **Contact & Professional Links:** Email, LinkedIn, GitHub, Vimeo, or relevant professional profiles.
6. **AI Collaboration & Ethics Note:** Footer, page, or appendix noting how AI tools were used across your work.

---

## The Professional Pitch (Week 14 Rehearsal / Week 15 Final)

* **Duration:** 3–5 minute live presentation supported by your portfolio site.
* **Content:** Introduce who you are, present your strategic positioning, walk through 1 key featured project, and summarize your immediate professional trajectory.
